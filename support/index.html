Support buzZza

A quiet way to help fund maintenance and future work, without turning the app into a subscription gate.

What support means here

Support is optional. It does not unlock features, remove limits, or change what the core app can do. The point is simply to help fund ongoing maintenance for something I want to keep useful over the long term.

Why there are two options

One-time support is for people who just want to chip in once. Recurring support is for people who want to help cover the ongoing work of maintaining and improving the app over time.

One-time support

A simple way to contribute once if buzZza has been useful to you.

Recurring support

A monthly option for people who want to help keep development moving.

Open recurring support

What will not change

The main buzZza experience is meant to stay available without monthly payments. If paid add-ons exist later, they should be optional extras like deeper insights, not a way of holding your basic records hostage.
