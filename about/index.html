About buzZza

A beekeeping log built to stay useful, stay honest, and stay out of your way.

What it is

buzZza is my attempt to build a beekeeping log I will actually keep up with. The goal is simple: make it easy to record what happened, find it later, and keep enough structure that the record stays useful over time.

The honest version

I am a better software engineer than beekeeper, which is a low bar, but I would rather be honest about that than pretend otherwise. buzZza is shaped by real beekeeping work, but also by the fact that I know how easy it is for note-taking systems to get too clever and stop being usable.

Why I am building it this way

I want the main experience to be something you can buy at a flat rate and keep using. If I do this right, the core logging workflow should not turn into another monthly bill just to keep your own records accessible.

What may come later

I expect to explore optional extras over time, especially insights, analysis, and other things that take ongoing work to maintain. If those show up, they should be add-ons, not a tollbooth in front of the basics.

Support

If buzZza is useful and you want to help fund maintenance, there is a support page. Support is optional and does not unlock functionality in the app.
